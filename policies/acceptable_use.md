# Acceptable Use Policy

Version 4.1. Effective 2026-01-15.

### AUP-1.1 Scope
<!-- data_types: customer, employee, financial, credential, internal_doc; channels: approved_tool, unsanctioned_tool, personal_email, personal_device, removable_media; disposition: restrictive -->
This policy governs all handling of company information by employees, contractors, and temporary staff, regardless of the device or service used. It applies whether the device is company-issued or personally owned.

### AUP-2.1 Approved Collaboration Tools
<!-- data_types: customer, employee, financial, internal_doc; channels: approved_tool; disposition: permissive -->
Employees may store, share, and process company information using tools on the approved tool register, which currently comprises the corporate Google Workspace tenant, the corporate Slack workspace, and the corporate instance of Jira. Sharing within these tools with other authenticated employees is permitted without additional approval, provided the employee is entitled to the data under the data classification policy.

### AUP-2.2 Internal Distribution
<!-- data_types: internal_doc; channels: approved_tool; disposition: permissive -->
Internal documents classified as Internal may be circulated freely among employees through approved tools. No further authorization is required.

### AUP-3.1 Personal Email and Personal Devices
<!-- data_types: customer, employee, financial, credential; channels: personal_email, personal_device; disposition: prohibitive -->
Employees must not transmit Confidential or Restricted information to a personal email account, store it on a personally owned device, or photograph it with a personal device. This prohibition applies even when the employee is the data subject and even when the transfer is intended to be temporary.

### AUP-3.2 Removable Media
<!-- data_types: customer, employee, financial, credential, internal_doc; channels: removable_media; disposition: prohibitive -->
Copying company information to USB drives, external disks, or other removable media is prohibited without written approval from the Information Security team.

### AUP-4.1 Credentials
<!-- data_types: credential; channels: approved_tool, unsanctioned_tool, personal_email, personal_device, removable_media; disposition: prohibitive -->
Passwords, API keys, access tokens, private certificates, and database connection strings must never be shared through any channel, including approved tools and messages to other employees. Credentials are distributed only through the corporate secrets manager.

### AUP-5.1 External Recipients
<!-- data_types: customer, employee, financial, internal_doc; channels: approved_tool; disposition: restrictive -->
Disclosure of company information to any party outside the company requires either an executed data processing agreement with that party or written approval from the Legal team. Approved vendors with an executed agreement are listed on the vendor register.

### AUP-5.2 Bulk Transfers
<!-- data_types: customer, employee, financial; channels: approved_tool, unsanctioned_tool; disposition: prohibitive -->
Transfers of more than 100 records of Confidential or Restricted information to any external recipient are prohibited without written approval from both the Information Security team and the Data Protection Officer, regardless of whether a data processing agreement is in place.
