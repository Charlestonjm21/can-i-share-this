# Data Classification Policy

Version 2.3. Effective 2025-09-01.

### DCP-1.1 Classification Levels
<!-- data_types: customer, employee, financial, credential, internal_doc, public; channels: approved_tool, unsanctioned_tool, personal_email, personal_device, removable_media; disposition: restrictive -->
Company information is classified at one of four levels. Public information may be disclosed without restriction. Internal information may be shared among employees. Confidential information is limited to employees with a business need. Restricted information is limited to named individuals and carries the controls described in this policy.

### DCP-2.1 Customer Records
<!-- data_types: customer; channels: approved_tool, unsanctioned_tool, personal_email, personal_device; disposition: restrictive -->
Customer names, contact details, account identifiers, purchase history, and support correspondence are classified Confidential. Customer payment information and government identifiers are classified Restricted. Customer records may be accessed only by employees in roles with a documented business need.

### DCP-2.2 Employee Records
<!-- data_types: employee; channels: approved_tool, unsanctioned_tool, personal_email, personal_device; disposition: restrictive -->
Employee personal data, compensation information, performance documentation, and applicant records are classified Confidential. Access is limited to the Human Resources function and to an employee's direct management chain. Employee medical and benefits information is classified Restricted.

### DCP-2.3 Financial Records
<!-- data_types: financial; channels: approved_tool, unsanctioned_tool; disposition: restrictive -->
Unpublished financial results, forecasts, budgets, and pricing models are classified Confidential until released. Bank account details and payment processor credentials are classified Restricted.

### DCP-2.4 Internal Documents
<!-- data_types: internal_doc; channels: approved_tool; disposition: permissive -->
Routine internal material such as meeting notes, process documentation, and team planning documents is classified Internal unless it contains information of a higher classification. Internal material may be shared among employees through approved tools without additional approval.

### DCP-3.1 Classification by Content
<!-- data_types: customer, employee, financial, internal_doc; channels: approved_tool, unsanctioned_tool, personal_email, personal_device, removable_media; disposition: restrictive -->
A document takes the classification of the most sensitive information it contains, regardless of its title or stated purpose. A document labeled as meeting notes that contains customer account identifiers is classified Confidential.
