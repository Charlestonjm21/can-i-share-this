# Third-Party AI Services Policy

Version 1.2. Effective 2026-03-01.

### AI-1.1 Scope
<!-- data_types: customer, employee, financial, credential, internal_doc, public; channels: unsanctioned_tool, approved_tool; disposition: restrictive -->
This policy governs the use of externally hosted artificial intelligence services, including general-purpose chat assistants, code assistants, transcription services, and image generation services, whether paid or free.

### AI-2.1 Approved AI Services
<!-- data_types: internal_doc, public; channels: approved_tool; disposition: permissive -->
The company maintains an enterprise agreement with a single approved AI assistant, available through the corporate single sign-on portal. Information classified Public or Internal may be submitted to the approved service. The enterprise agreement provides that submitted content is not retained for model training.

### AI-3.1 Consumer AI Services
<!-- data_types: customer, employee, financial, credential, internal_doc; channels: unsanctioned_tool; disposition: prohibitive -->
Employees must not submit company information to consumer or free-tier AI services, including the public versions of ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, and comparable tools. These services operate without a data processing agreement and their retention terms are outside the company's control. This prohibition covers prompts, pasted text, uploaded files, screenshots, and code.

### AI-3.2 Confidential and Restricted Information
<!-- data_types: customer, employee, financial, credential; channels: approved_tool, unsanctioned_tool; disposition: prohibitive -->
Information classified Confidential or Restricted must not be submitted to any AI service, including the approved service, without written approval from the Data Protection Officer. This restriction exists because AI services process content in ways the company cannot audit at the record level.

### AI-4.1 Output Handling
<!-- data_types: internal_doc; channels: approved_tool; disposition: restrictive -->
Output generated by an approved AI service is classified at the same level as the information submitted to produce it. Employees remain responsible for verifying the accuracy of AI-generated content before using it in company work.

### AI-5.1 Requesting a New Service
<!-- data_types: public, internal_doc; channels: unsanctioned_tool; disposition: restrictive -->
Employees who wish to use an AI service not on the approved register must submit a request to the Information Security team. Use of the service is not permitted while the request is pending.
